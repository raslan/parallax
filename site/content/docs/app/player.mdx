Parallax has a built-in player, so you can check a file without opening another app. The same player is used everywhere you can play something: Files, Duplicates, Cleanup, Compress, Subtitles, Organize and the audio pages.

<Shot id="app/player/open" />

## Opening and closing

Click a video, or use **Play** from its menu. It opens in a window over the page, with the file name above it and details such as size, length and format below. Close it with the **×**.

## Controls

The controls appear when you move the mouse over the player: play and pause, volume, the time, a bar to skip around, a settings menu, picture in picture and fullscreen.

Parallax remembers your volume and whether you muted it, so it is the same the next time you play something.

## Subtitles

If subtitle files sit next to the video, the player finds them when it opens. Open the settings menu and choose **Captions** to switch them on or pick a language. Subtitles in `.srt` and other common formats are shown as they are.

To get subtitles for videos that do not have any, see [Subtitles](/docs/tools/subtitles).

## Sound that browsers cannot play

Some videos use a sound format that browsers cannot play, which is common with files from Blu-ray discs. Parallax converts just the sound as you watch and leaves the picture alone. The video starts after a couple of seconds, and you can still skip to any point. Your file is never changed.

The technical details are in [Playback and audio conversion](/docs/advanced/playback).

## Audio files

Audio files use the same player, without a picture. On the Audio pages, press the play button on a row.
