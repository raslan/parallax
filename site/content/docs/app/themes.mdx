Parallax is a dark app, and you can pick the accent and the shade of dark that you like best. There are sixteen themes in three groups.

<Shot id="app/themes/picker" />

## Changing theme

Open **Settings → General**. The **Colour theme** card shows every theme as a small preview. Click one and it takes effect straight away.

You can also switch from the [command palette](/docs/app/command-palette) by searching for a theme's name, which is quicker if you want to try a few.

The theme is saved in Parallax itself, not in your browser. Every device you open Parallax on gets the same one, and there is no flash of a different colour when the page loads.

## The themes

**Parallax set.** Neutral dark greys where only the accent changes.

- **Parallax** is the default. Very dark, with a strong indigo accent.
- **Elegance** has a warm gold accent on a deep blue-grey background.
- **Graphite** is a neutral grey with a soft teal accent.
- **Rose Quartz** is near-black with a pink accent.
- **OLED** is pure black with a white accent, for screens where black saves power.

**Colour set.** The background itself is tinted.

- **Deep Space** is purple.
- **Modern HUD** is cyan.
- **Neon Grid** is green.

**Classic palettes.** Well-known colour schemes from code editors.

- **Catppuccin Mocha**, **Dracula**, **Nord**, **Tokyo Night**, **Gruvbox Dark**, **Solarized Dark**, **One Dark** and **Rose Pine**.

There is no light mode.
