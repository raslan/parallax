Open Settings from the cog in the top bar. It has six tabs. Each tab has its own **Save changes** button, so changes you make on one tab are not saved until you press it on that tab. (The theme is the exception. It applies as soon as you click it.)

## General

- **Colour theme** picks the look of the app. See [Themes](/docs/app/themes).
- **Concurrent jobs** is how many jobs of any kind run at once across the whole app. The default is 1. See [Jobs](/docs/app/jobs).
- **Statistics** has **Clear statistics**, which wipes the totals on the Home page. It does not touch your libraries or files. See [Home and statistics](/docs/app/home).
- **Danger Zone** has **Purge all library data**, with a confirmation. It removes every library, the list of files, thumbnails and the other things Parallax keeps about them. Your settings, downloaded AI models and statistics stay, and **your files on disk are not touched**. You can add libraries again afterwards.

## Transcoder

- **Concurrent video transcodes (per GPU)** is how many files each graphics card works on at once during Compress and Toolbox. Without a graphics card it applies to the processor.
- **Concurrent audio transcodes** is how many audio files are processed at once. Audio always uses the processor.

The text on this tab also tells you which graphics cards Parallax found. See [Hardware acceleration](/docs/advanced/hardware-acceleration).

## Keys & Accounts

- **TMDB API key** is needed to match TV shows and movies in [Identify](/docs/tools/identify), and for the movie source in Subtitles search. A free key from the TMDB website is all it takes.
- **Subtitle sync engine** chooses between **alass** and **ffsubsync** for fixing subtitles that are out of time. alass is the default.
- **Auto-sync downloaded subtitles** runs the sync automatically after each subtitle is downloaded.

## AI Models

- **Scan batch size** is how many images are checked in one go by Content Review. Higher is faster and uses more memory.
- **Scan prefetch** is how many batches are loaded ahead of time while the current one is being checked. Higher keeps things moving and uses more memory.
- **Content detection models** lists the available models. The standard one is built in. The larger one can be downloaded here, and you choose which one to use.

## Downloads

- **Default download directory** is where [Downloads](/docs/tools/downloads) saves things unless you pick another folder on the page.
- **Max concurrent downloads** is how many links download at the same time.
- The download tool itself is installed and updated here, and you can follow the **stable** or **nightly** release channel.
