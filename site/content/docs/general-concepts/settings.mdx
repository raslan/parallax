Open Settings from the cog in the top bar. It has eight tabs. Each tab has its own **Save changes** button, so changes you make on one tab are not saved until you press it on that tab. (The theme is the exception. It applies as soon as you click it.)

## General

- **Colour theme** picks the look of the app. See [Themes](/docs/general-concepts/themes).
- **Concurrent jobs** is how many jobs of any kind run at once across the whole app. The default is 1. See [Jobs](/docs/general-concepts/jobs).
- **Account** changes your username or password, and has **Sign out everywhere**. It is hidden if sign-in is turned off. See [Account and sign-in](/docs/general-concepts/account).
- **Statistics** has **Clear statistics**, which wipes the totals on the Home page. It does not touch your libraries or files. See [Home and statistics](/docs/general-concepts/home).
- **Danger Zone** has **Purge all library data**, with a confirmation. It removes every library, the list of files, thumbnails and the other things Parallax keeps about them. Your settings, downloaded AI models and statistics stay, and **your files on disk are not touched**. You can add libraries again afterwards.

## Transcoder

- **Concurrent video transcodes (per GPU)** is how many files each graphics card works on at once during Compress and Toolbox. Without a graphics card it applies to the processor.
- **Concurrent audio transcodes** is how many audio files are processed at once. Audio always uses the processor.

The text on this tab also tells you which graphics cards Parallax found. See [Hardware acceleration](/docs/advanced/hardware-acceleration).

## Subtitles

- **Default subtitle languages** are the languages the [Subtitles](/docs/tools/subtitles) page looks for and downloads. English is selected to start with.
- **Subtitle sync engine** chooses between **alass** and **ffsubsync** for fixing subtitles that are out of time. alass is the default.
- **Auto-sync downloaded subtitles** runs the sync automatically after each subtitle is downloaded.
- **Leave out sound notes and speaker names when translating** is off by default. Subtitles for the hard of hearing carry lines like `[DISTANT EXPLOSION]` and names like `FURY:`. Parallax translates them and keeps them. Tick this box to drop them from translated subtitles.
- **Language packs** lists the languages Parallax can translate into without leaving your computer. Arabic is the first one. **Install** downloads it (about 460 MB) and checks it, and the bin button removes it again. Each row names the model it comes from and its licence. See [Translating a subtitle](/docs/tools/subtitles#translating-a-subtitle).

- **Speech-to-text models** lists the two models that can write a subtitle from a video's audio: **Whisper small** (about 460 MB, the one to start with) and **Whisper medium** (about 1.5 GB, slower, and better with names). **Install** downloads and checks one, and the bin button removes it. See [Generating a subtitle from the audio](/docs/tools/subtitles#generating-a-subtitle-from-the-audio).

## Keys & Accounts

- **TMDB API key** is needed to match TV shows and movies in [Identify](/docs/tools/identify), and for the movie source in Subtitles search. A free key from the TMDB website is all it takes.

## AI Models

- **Scan batch size** is how many images are checked in one go by Content Review. Higher is faster and uses more memory.
- **Scan prefetch** is how many batches are loaded ahead of time while the current one is being checked. Higher keeps things moving and uses more memory.
- **Content Detection (NudeNet)** lists the available models. The standard one (**320n**) is built in. The larger one (**640m**) can be downloaded here with **Download**, and **Use** chooses which one is active. See [Content detection](/docs/advanced/content-detection).

## Hardware

- **Detected graphics cards** lists what Parallax found.
- **NVIDIA acceleration package** is a download of about 2 GB that lets translation and speech-to-text use an NVIDIA card. **Install**, **Update**, **Check** (a tiny test) and the bin button to remove it.
- **Use GPU for translation and speech-to-text** turns it on or off. It is on once the package is installed.
- **GPUs to use** is how many cards the jobs may use. It shows when you have more than one NVIDIA card. Each file runs on one card.

Video encoding with a graphics card is set on the Transcoder tab, and is not changed here. See [Hardware acceleration](/docs/advanced/hardware-acceleration#translation-and-speech-to-text-on-an-nvidia-card).

## Downloads

- **Default download directory** is where [Downloads](/docs/tools/downloads) saves things unless you pick another folder on the page.
- **Max concurrent downloads** is how many links download at the same time.
- The download tool itself is installed here with **Install** (and brought up to date with **Update**). **Release channel** lets you follow the **stable** or **nightly** release.
