The command palette is a search box that takes you anywhere in Parallax without clicking through the menus.

<Shot id="app/palette/open" />

## Opening it

Press **Ctrl+K** (or **⌘K** on a Mac), or click the search button in the top bar. Type a few letters, use the arrow keys to pick, and press Enter. Escape closes it.

## What it can do

The palette lists:

- Every page in the app, grouped by the section it lives in: Videos, Images, Audio and Tools.
- **Home**, **Jobs** and **Settings** under **Actions**.
- Every colour theme under **Theme**. Picking one switches to it straight away.

It matches as you type, so `comp` finds both Compress pages, and `dup` finds the Duplicates pages.
