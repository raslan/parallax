Anything that takes a while in Parallax runs as a job in the background, so the app stays usable while it works. Scans, compressing, duplicate searches, subtitle downloads, upscaling and more all show up here.

<Shot id="getting-started/concepts/jobs" />

## Where to look

There are two places.

- The **Jobs** page, from the pulse icon in the top bar or the command palette, lists everything. Running and waiting jobs are at the top and finished ones are in **History**.
- The pulse icon in the top bar opens a small menu with the same list, so you can check on things without leaving the page you are on. A badge on it counts active jobs.

Parallax also pops up a message when a job finishes, and the number of active jobs appears in the browser tab's title so you can see it from another tab.

## What you can do with a job

Each job shows its type, how far along it is, the file it is working on and how long ago it finished. Click a job to open its **log**, which is the best place to look when something goes wrong. A job that failed opens its log by itself.

You can **cancel** a job that is running or waiting. A job that is waiting for its turn is simply removed from the queue. **Clear history** removes finished jobs from the list.

## How many run at once

By default Parallax runs one job at a time, and the rest wait their turn in the order they were started. To run more together, raise **Concurrent jobs** in **Settings → General**.

That setting is about whole jobs. How many files a single Compress or Toolbox job handles at once is a separate setting in **Settings → Transcoder**. The [Hardware acceleration](/docs/advanced/hardware-acceleration) page explains the difference.

## After a restart

If Parallax is stopped while a job is running, that job is marked as failed the next time it starts, so nothing looks like it is still running when it is not.
