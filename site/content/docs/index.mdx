---
title: What is Parallax?
description: A self-hosted app for looking after your video, image and audio collections.
---

Parallax is a free, open source app for looking after a big media collection. It runs in Docker on your own machine and you use it from a web browser. It sits alongside your media player and looks after the files behind it.

New here? Start with [Install](/docs/getting-started/install), then [Your first scan](/docs/getting-started/first-scan). If something goes wrong, [Troubleshooting](/docs/getting-started/troubleshooting) covers the common problems.
