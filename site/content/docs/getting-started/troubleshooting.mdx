---
title: Troubleshooting and FAQ
description: Quick answers to the problems people run into most often.
---

## The page will not load

Check that the container is running with `docker compose ps`, and look at its messages with `docker compose logs`. If you changed the port, remember the left number in the `ports` line is the one you open in the browser.

## I cannot see my folders when adding a library

The folder picker only shows what the container can see. Make sure the folder is listed under `volumes` in your compose file, then run `docker compose up -d` again. Your media shows up inside the container wherever you put the right-hand side of that line, for example `/media`.

## Files made by Parallax belong to the wrong user

Set `user:` in your compose file to your own user and group numbers. Run `id` in a terminal to find them, then restart the container.

## A library says it has no files

New libraries only fill up once they are scanned. Click the scan button on the library's card (hover it to see **Scan for new / removed files**). If the scan finishes and it is still empty, check the folder really has video, image or audio files in it, and that Parallax is allowed to read it.

## A video plays with no sound in the browser

Some audio formats, like the ones used on many Blu-ray rips, cannot be played by browsers. Parallax converts the sound on the fly when you press play, so the video starts after a couple of seconds and then plays normally. Your file is not changed.

## Compression is slow

Compressing video is heavy work, and without a graphics card it all runs on your processor. If you have a graphics card, you can give it to Docker. Look under **Settings → Transcoder** to see whether Parallax found one, and read [Hardware acceleration](/docs/advanced/hardware-acceleration).

## Thumbnails take a moment to show up

Thumbnails are made when you first look at them, and a background job fills in the rest after a scan. Large libraries can take a little while.

## Identify cannot find my show

Matching TV shows and movies needs a free TMDB key. Create one on the TMDB website and paste it under **Settings → Keys & Accounts**. Use the short key called **API Key**, not the very long **API Read Access Token**. Custom mode in Identify works without any key.

## Where does Parallax keep its data?

In the `data` folder next to your compose file. That has your libraries, settings and thumbnails. Your actual media is never copied in there.

## Sign-in tells me to wait

After ten wrong passwords in a row, Parallax makes you wait up to a minute before the next try. A correct password does not skip the wait. Restart the container (`docker restart parallax`) to clear it at once. See [Account and sign-in](/docs/general-concepts/account).

## I forgot my password

Run `docker exec -it parallax python -m app.reset_password` on the machine where Parallax runs. It asks for a new password and signs every browser out. See [Account and sign-in](/docs/general-concepts/account#if-you-forget-your-password).

## It still is not working

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/raslan/parallax/issues) and include the output of `docker compose logs` so it is easier to see what happened.
